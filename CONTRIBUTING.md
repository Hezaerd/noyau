# Contributing

## Read This First

Contributions are welcome. We are picky about them.

This project is still early. We want outside help, and we also want to keep scope, quality, and direction under control. A sloppy, unfocused, or speculative PR will be closed.

Bugs and feature requests belong in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hezaerd/noyau/issues).

## What We Are Most Likely To Accept

Small, focused bug fixes.

Small reliability fixes.

Small performance improvements.

Tightly scoped maintenance or documentation work that clearly improves the project without changing its direction.

## What We Are Least Likely To Accept

Large PRs.

Drive-by feature work.

Opinionated rewrites.

Anything that expands product scope without an issue first.

If you open a 1,000+ line PR full of new features, we will close it quickly and remember that you ignored these instructions.

## If You Want To Open A PR

Keep it small.

Explain exactly what changed.

Explain exactly why the change should exist.

Do not mix unrelated fixes together.

If the PR makes anything resembling a UI change, include clear before/after images.

If the change depends on motion, timing, transitions, or interaction details, include a short video.

If we have to guess what changed, we are much less likely to review it.

Use the [Vite+](https://vite.plus) CLI (`vp`) for install, scripts, and checks. Do not use `bun run`.

## Open an Issue First

If you are thinking about a non-trivial change, open an issue first.

That still does not mean we will want the PR, but it gives you a chance to avoid wasting your time.

## Be Realistic

A PR is a proposal, not a claim on our time.

We may ask you to shrink it, change the approach, or close it and start over. We may reimplement the idea ourselves later.

If the work is tight and the why is clear, we will look at it.
