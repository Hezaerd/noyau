# Open where you left off

Noyau reopens the Board, Thread, or new Thread you last had open.

## Open Noyau again

Launch the app, or open it at the home address. After the splash, you land on the Board, Thread, or new Thread you were using, in that Project.

Settings do not come back as the first screen. If you were in Settings, Noyau reopens the Board or Thread you had open before that.

## If that screen is gone

- When the Thread is gone, Noyau opens that Project's Board.
- When the Project is gone, Noyau opens the empty home so you can link a folder.

To land somewhere else next time, open that Board or Thread before you quit.
